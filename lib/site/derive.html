Deriving Ligurian

Sauleis Bazdā (Solar Speech) and Mazdō-yō Heardō̃ (Heart Speech), by rule

Every form on this page is computed live by the system's own SCArs engine (its rewriting core runs in Latte on Loom): PIE → Solar Speech applies the grammar's §II table (lib/pie.sca, stages 0a–9), and Solar → Heart Speech applies the Vulgar-Ligurian changes (lib/ligurian.sca, Phases II–V) plus the stress-conditioned breaking and nasalization passes. Nothing is looked up — type a word and it is derived.

Derive words

Enter words separated by spaces, then choose the starting stage. PIE roots may use laryngeals and the usual notation: try dʰeh₁s mr̥to ǵʰel gʷih₃w nokʷt srew h₂stér sāwel weseti — or Solar words: liga nivo mazda saules bazdā serdā tekstā senti pewtā

Generate vocabulary

The generator (lib/lexis.lat, written in Latte) builds phonotactically well-formed Solar Speech roots — §I onsets, nuclei and codas — from a seeded pseudo-random stream, and SCArs immediately derives each word's Heart Speech form. The same seed always gives the same vocabulary.

The rule files ship with the system as lib/pie.sca and lib/ligurian.sca, and the same derivations run from the command line: latte sca --file lib/pie.sca dʰeh₁s, latte evolve saules. The complete grammar is at /grammar; the sound-change engine's documentation is at /docs.
